收棍|Sheathe|X|R / MMB

防御|Guard|Hold RB|Hold E

切换短棍|Select short reach|RB + B|E + RMB
#地面攻击|Ground attacks

特殊连击|Special string|Tap B repeatedly|Tap RMB repeatedly

EX蓄力突刺|EX thrust & shockwave|Hold Y, add RB in charge window|Hold LMB, add E in charge window
冲刺长棍攻击|Running long strike|Y in long-reach sprint|LMB in long-reach sprint
冲刺短棍攻击|Running short strike|Y in short-reach sprint|LMB in short-reach sprint
冲刺特殊攻击|Running special strike|B during sprint|RMB during sprint
